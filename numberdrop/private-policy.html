Privacy Policy

App: NumberDrop  |  Effective date: June 16, 2026  |  Last updated: June 16, 2026

1. Introduction

NumberDrop is a free mobile puzzle game for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

By installing or using NumberDrop, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

NumberDrop itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to any server operated by the developer.

However, NumberDrop displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. As part of delivering these ads, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect the following data from your device:

  • Android Advertising ID (a resettable identifier used for ad targeting)
  • Device identifiers (e.g., device model, OS version)
  • IP address (used to infer general location such as country or region)
  • App usage data (e.g., which screens were viewed, session duration)
  • Ad interaction data (e.g., whether an ad was viewed or clicked)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by the developer of NumberDrop.

3. How This Information Is Used

The information collected by Google AdMob is used to:

  • Serve relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance and frequency capping
  • Detect and prevent ad fraud
  • Provide aggregate analytics to the developer (non-personal, e.g., total impressions)

The developer of NumberDrop does not use this data for any purpose beyond receiving aggregated, anonymised advertising revenue reports from AdMob.

4. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

NumberDrop stores the following data only on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.
  • Your best score
  • Your recent game scores (last 5 games)
  • Your preferred display language

This data is stored locally to remember your preferences between sessions. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.

5. Third-Party Services

NumberDrop uses the following third-party services that may collect data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — Ad serving platform.
    Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services — Required by the Android platform.
    Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Your Rights and Choices

You can opt out of interest-based advertising or reset your Android Advertising ID at any time:

  • Android 12 and later: Go to Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"
  • Older Android versions: Go to Google Settings → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" and "Reset advertising ID"

Resetting or deleting your Advertising ID means Google AdMob can no longer use it to show you personalised ads. You may still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

7. Children's Privacy

NumberDrop is not directed at children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us (see Section 9) and we will take steps to delete that information.

Because the app uses AdMob, it is not designed for use by children under 13 in jurisdictions covered by COPPA (United States) or similar laws.

8. Data Retention

The developer does not retain any personal data on any server. Local data (scores and language preference) is retained on your device until you uninstall the app. Google's data retention practices are described in Google's Privacy Policy.
