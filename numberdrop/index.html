How to Play

Learn in under a minute

1

Tiles fall from the top

A new numbered tile spawns at the top-centre every turn.

2

Swipe to move & drop

Swipe left or right to steer the tile. Swipe down to drop it instantly.

3

Match to merge

Two tiles with the same number in the same column merge into one with double the value.

4

Survive & score

The game ends when a new tile can't spawn because the column is already full. Keep merging to stay alive!
